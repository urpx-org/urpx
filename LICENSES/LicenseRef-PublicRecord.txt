LicenseRef-PublicRecord — US Public Record

This license reference identifier denotes documents that are public record
under US federal or state law and are therefore not subject to copyright
restrictions on inclusion or redistribution.

The file(s) this identifier is attached to in REUSE.toml are included in
the URPX repository solely as test-case assets. They are regulatory filings
or published tariff schedules made available by a US utility or regulatory
body in the ordinary course of its public-facing duties. Such documents
are not the copyrightable creative expression of any party; they are
factual public records of regulatory processes.

No warranty is made by the URPX authors regarding the accuracy,
completeness, or currency of any public-record document included here.
Users who require an authoritative copy must consult the issuing utility
or regulator directly.

This LicenseRef identifier is provided per the SPDX specification so that
automated license scanners (ScanCode, FOSSology) can cleanly classify
public-record content without surfacing false-positive copyright findings.
