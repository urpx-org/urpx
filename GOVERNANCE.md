# Governance

URPX is developed by the **URPX Working Group**, hosted under **LF Energy Standards and Specifications (LFESS)**, a Linux Foundation project. The working group charter designates the working group as the body responsible for promoting URPX materials through the LFESS stages (Sandbox → Draft → Approved).

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Working Group** | Everyone participating in URPX's development: attends meetings, reviews proposals, and discusses design questions. Open to anyone who joins the mailing list. |
| **Committee** | Votes on stage promotions and releases. A smaller group than the working group; see [Committee membership](#committee-membership) below. |
| **Chair** | Convenes and runs working group meetings, coordinates the review and release process, and represents the project toward LF Energy. |
| **Maintainers** | Review and merge changes to this repository. Listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). |

## Decision making

- Day-to-day changes (documentation fixes, test data, clearly scoped improvements) are handled through pull requests reviewed by a maintainer. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for expectations.
- Structural changes to the ontology, SHACL shapes, or repository layout require working group discussion before implementation, normally at a working group meeting or in a focused GitHub issue.
- Stage promotions and the decision to publish are made by the committee and recorded in the meeting minutes. The committee may decide by recorded consensus at a meeting or by an electronic ballot, as the circumstances warrant; a formal ballot is not required in every case. The license consequences of publication are described in [TRANSITION.md](TRANSITION.md).

## Meetings and participation

Working group meetings are open to anyone. Join the mailing list, [urpx-wg@lists.lfenergy.org](https://lists.lfenergy.org/g/urpx-wg) (a public, self-service subscription), to receive the meeting calendar; an LFX account gets you the Zoom link. Agendas and minutes are published in the [urpx-wg repository](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx-wg).

## Committee membership

Attending meetings and joining the working group mailing list is open to anyone. Voting on stage promotions and releases is reserved to the committee, a smaller group reachable at urpx-committee@lists.lfenergy.org.

To request committee membership: contact the chair at `chair@urpx.org` or at a working group meeting, having attended at least one working group meeting or made a contribution or proposal. The chair adds new members and notes the addition in the next meeting's minutes.

## Changes to governance

Changes to this document require working group approval.
